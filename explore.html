Compare Your Own Vitals

Where do you sit in this cohort?

Enter age, blood pressure, blood glucose, and resting heart rate below, and this page compares those values two ways. First, against the 1014 pregnancies in the dataset: a k-nearest-neighbors lookup standardizes your inputs the same way the cohort’s vitals are (centered on the cohort mean, scaled by the cohort SD), and the 15 closest cohort records by Euclidean distance vote on a risk level. Second, your blood pressure reading is checked against diagnostic thresholds from a 2022 American Heart Association scientific statement on hypertension in pregnancy — independent of this dataset, and grounded in the published clinical literature. Everything runs in your browser — nothing you enter is sent anywhere. 

# slim table + standardization stats for the k-NN lookup below
# (same standardization used in report.Rmd)
comparison_vars = c("age", "systolic_bp", "diastolic_bp", "bs", "heart_rate")

cohort_slim = maternal_df |>
  select(all_of(comparison_vars), risk_level) |>
  mutate(risk_level = as.character(risk_level))

cohort_stats = maternal_df |>
  summarise(across(all_of(comparison_vars), list(mean = mean, sd = sd), .names = "{.col}__{.fn}"))

cohort_json = toJSON(cohort_slim, dataframe = "rows", auto_unbox = TRUE)
stats_json  = toJSON(as.list(cohort_stats), auto_unbox = TRUE)

This tool is descriptive, not diagnostic. The cohort comparison above tells you which historical records in this dataset your entered vitals most resemble; the blood-pressure category is drawn from published clinical guideline thresholds, not a diagnosis. Neither is a medical assessment, and neither was built or validated for clinical use. If you have concerns about a pregnancy, talk to a clinician.

Standardization and neighbor lookup mirror the modeling approach in the report. See the home page for variable definitions and the data source.

Blood-pressure category thresholds cited above are from Garovic VD, Dechend R, Easterling T, et al. Hypertension in Pregnancy: Diagnosis, Blood Pressure Goals, and Pharmacotherapy: A Scientific Statement From the American Heart Association. Hypertension. 2022;79(2):e21–e41. doi:10.1161/HYP.0000000000000208.
